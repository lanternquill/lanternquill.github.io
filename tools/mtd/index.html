Do I need Making Tax Digital for Income Tax?

A free, private checker for UK sole traders and landlords: find out when you need to start, then see your quarterly deadlines. Nothing you type leaves this page.

1. When do I need to start?

Enter your qualifying income: self-employment plus property income before expenses (turnover). Leave out wages (PAYE), dividends, pensions and partnership profit shares. For a jointly owned property, enter your share only.

Tax year 2026 to 2027 (estimate)

Thresholds from GOV.UK: more than £50,000 in 2024-25 → start 6 April 2026; more than £30,000 in 2025-26 → 6 April 2027; more than £20,000 in 2026-27 → 6 April 2028. Some people are exempt (for example if digitally excluded). Check on GOV.UK.

2. My quarterly deadlines

Each quarterly update covers from the start of the tax year to the end of the period. Use calendar periods if your accounting period ends on 31 March.
